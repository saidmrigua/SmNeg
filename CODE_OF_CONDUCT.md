# Code of conduct

SMneg is a small, friendly project for photographers and developers. We expect everyone who takes part in
issues, pull requests and discussions to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to film scanning or to programming;
- give feedback on the work, not on the person;
- accept that questions are welcome and that not every request can be built;
- not harass, insult, discriminate against or publish private information about anyone.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people, who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's
"Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainer through the profile at
[github.com/saidmrigua](https://github.com/saidmrigua).

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
